Economy & Labour

Local
The Central Coast labour market reflects both regional dynamics and the national monetary policy cycle. Unemployment and JobSeeker recipient volumes are sensitive leading indicators of economic stress, while retail spend tracks consumer confidence in real time.

Housing & Construction

Local
The Central Coast housing market is shaped by sea-change migration, constrained land supply, and the interest rate cycle. Residential vacancy rates below 1% since 2021 have kept upward pressure on rents, while building approvals signal future supply conditions.

Transport & Mobility

Local
Vehicle registration data tracks both consumer confidence and fleet renewal. Total, private and business registrations are available from Transport for NSW. Note: EV-specific registration data is not yet included in the source spreadsheet — it will be added in the next data refresh once the EV breakdown column is available.

Tourism & Visitor Economy

Local
The Central Coast's visitor economy is driven by domestic drive markets, proximity to Sydney, and a significant short-term rental (STRA) sector. Revenue and occupancy metrics from the STRA sector provide a near real-time view of visitor demand.
